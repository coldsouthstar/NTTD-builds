Delete your NTTD account

How to remove your NTTD account and the information stored for it.

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open NTTD and tap the account button at the top of the menu.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete my online account.
  3. Choose Delete online account, or Delete online account and this phone's data.

This deletes your account, progress, saved stats, ghost, daily board entries, challenge codes, match history and reports straight away. It can't be undone.

If you can't open the game

Email fullfruntall@yomiyokai.com from the email address saved on your account, with the subject "Delete my NTTD account". We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm. If you never saved an email, uninstalling the game removes everything on your phone, and the unused guest account is deleted automatically after 12 months.

What we keep

Nothing linked to you. A moderation log entry saying an account was deleted (without your details) is kept for up to 12 months.

Start fresh instead

To wipe your progress but keep your account, use Start fresh in the same screen.
