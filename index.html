Nine TapzTill Death

A 1v1 tapping fighter for Android. Break their boxes, draw your spells, win the Nine Count, land the finisher.

Test it on Google Play Join the Discord Free test build for Android. Online play is for 13 and over.
CPU · Level 1 · Easy
You

Race the CPU: tap 1 to start, then 2 to 9.

How a fight goes

Break their grid

Tap the nine boxes on their side. Break them in number order to build a chain, keep tapping to build a combo.

Draw your spells

Fire, Water, Air and Earth are shapes you draw on the screen. Cast two or three in a row to fuse them.

Win the Nine Count

Break through their last grid and it's a race: tap 1 to 9 (or 9 to 1) faster than them.

Land the finisher

Draw the Final Sigil. The more you earned, the more elements it uses, up to a full NTTD Ultimate.

Join the test

On Google Play

  1. On your Android phone, join the NTTD tester group with the Google account your phone uses.
  2. Open the tester sign-up page and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install NTTD from the Play Store. From then on the Play Store keeps it updated.
  4. Stay signed up and play now and then: Google needs testers for 14 days in a row before NTTD can go public.

Or install the APK

  1. Tap Download for Android, then open the file. Only get the APK from this link, never from a copy someone sends you.
  2. For extra safety, let your phone check it first. When Play Protect asks, tap Scan app and wait for the result. It may also say it doesn't recognise the developer, because NTTD is new; once the scan is clean, tap Install anyway.
  3. Use one version or the other: they're signed differently, so to switch, uninstall the one you have first.
