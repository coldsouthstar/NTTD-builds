Privacy policy

For NTTD (Nine Tapz Till Death). Updated 23 November 2026.

Who we are

NTTD is made by Yomi Yokai, an independent game developer in the UK ("we"). We decide how the information below is used, so we're responsible for it under UK data protection law. Contact: fullfruntall@yomiyokai.com, or the NTTD Discord server.

Most of the game stays on your phone. Information is kept online only for players aged 13 and over who play online.

Who we share it with

Other players see your fighter name, archetype, level, record and scores, never your email. Supabase hosts our database and accounts in Ireland (EU), and only processes the information for us. The UK recognises the EU as giving the same level of protection, so no extra safeguards are needed. Our email provider sends the codes you ask for. Our Discord server is run under Discord's own privacy policy. We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

Your account and everything linked to it: until you delete it. Guest accounts nobody has used: up to 12 months. Reports and the moderation log: up to 12 months.

Children

The first time you open NTTD, it asks which age group you're in and keeps only that answer, on your phone. Under 13, the game plays offline: no account is made, nothing is sent to us, and Street Pass stays off. 13 to 17-year-olds get the same game as adults, with nothing extra collected. We follow the ICO's Children's code.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, to stop us using it, or for a copy. Email fullfruntall@yomiyokai.com; we'll reply within one month. You can delete everything yourself in the game: here's how. If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

We'll update this page and the date at the top when anything changes.
