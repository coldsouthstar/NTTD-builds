A free test build

NTTD is free and still being made. Expect bugs. Features change, and progress, levels, ratings or boards may be reset. The game is provided as it is, without promises that it will always work or always be available.

Age and accounts

Online play (accounts, ghosts, boards, challenges, live matches and Street Pass) is for players aged 13 and over. Under 13, the game plays offline. Keep your password to yourself; you're responsible for what happens on your account. You can delete your account at any time.

Play fair, be decent

  • No offensive, hateful or sexual fighter names, and no names that pretend to be someone else.
  • No harassment, threats, bullying or abuse of other players, in the game or on our Discord.
  • Don't share anyone's personal information, including your own.
  • No cheating, hacking, bots, or tampering with the game or its server.
  • Don't use the game for anything illegal.

If you break these rules we may replace or hide your fighter name, hide your ghost and board entries, reset your scores, or remove your account. Serious illegal content is reported to the relevant authorities.

Reporting and complaints

In the game, use ⚑ Report after a fight with someone's ghost or a live opponent, or next to a name on the daily board. Three reports about one name from different players hide it straight away, until a moderator checks it. On Discord, use Discord's Report option or message a Dev. By email: fullfruntall@yomiyokai.com. If your name or account was hidden or removed and you think we got it wrong, email us and we'll look again.
