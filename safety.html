Health and safety

Photosensitive seizures

A very small number of people may have a seizure when they see flashing lights or patterns, even with no history of epilepsy. NTTD keeps flashes to 3 a second or fewer, and you can lower effects in Settings. If you or anyone in your family has had a seizure, check with a doctor before playing.

Stop playing straight away and get medical advice if you feel dizzy, your vision changes, your eyes or muscles twitch, you lose awareness, feel confused or have any movement you can't control.

Take breaks

Fast tapping is hard on hands and wrists. Rest for a few minutes every hour, and stop if your hands, wrists or eyes hurt.
